Teacher's manual

1. How it works

You start a session and show its QR code on the classroom screen. Students scan it with their phone camera and sign in with their institution account. Each check-in records the student's name, email and time. It appears on your screen straight away.

The QR code changes every {{ st.qr_interval }} seconds. A photo of the code sent to a friend outside the room soon stops working, and students can't type in someone else's email.

2. Signing in

  • Click Sign in and choose Continue with Google. {% if domains %}Use your {{ domains }} account only. Other accounts are refused.{% endif %}
  • You see the teacher dashboard if an administrator has added your address as a teacher. Otherwise you see a student page: ask the administrator to add you, then reload the page.
  • Switch between English and ไทย at the top of any page.

3. Connecting Gmail

Click Connect Gmail on the dashboard once and allow sending email. Check-in confirmations to students and your attendance reports are then sent from your own address. You can disconnect at any time. This step is shown only if the server is set up to send through Gmail.

4. Starting a class session

  1. Class name is optional. Names you used before are suggested as you type.
  2. Mid-class checks: how many extra checks during class (0–3). You can add more while teaching.
  3. Check-out at the end of class: adds a final check. You can also add it later.
  4. Email each student a confirmation and Email me the attendance list when I close the session.
  5. Click Start and show QR code. The check-in opens immediately.

5. The QR display

  • Put this page on the projector. Use Full screen or press F.
  • The bar under the code shows when it will change. A code scanned just before it changes still works.
  • The list on the right shows who has checked in, newest at the top, with the count in large numbers.
  • Pause (or the Space bar) stops check-in immediately. The code is hidden and scans are refused. Resume starts it again.
  • The dot near the bottom right is green while the screen is connected live. If it turns red, it reconnects by itself. Reloading the page is always safe.
  • List opens the attendance list in a new tab.

6. Mid-class checks and check-out

  • The buttons at the top of the display show every check in the session and how many people are in each.
  • Click a check that hasn't started (or a closed one) to open it. Opening a check closes the one before it, so only one check is open at a time.
  • + Mid-class check and + Check-out add a new check and open it straight away. Check-out is always kept last.
  • Students scan the same screen again for every check.

7. Closing a session

Click Close session when class ends. Students can no longer check in. If you chose it, the attendance list is emailed to you as a table in the message, which is easy to paste into a spreadsheet or an AI assistant. You are taken to the attendance list.

8. Attendance list and corrections

  • Open a session from the dashboard to see one row per student with their time for each check. A red dash means they missed that check.
  • Download CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets, Thai names included. Email me this list sends it again.
  • Corrections → Add by hand: add a student who couldn't scan, for example a phone with a flat battery. Choose which check to add them to.
  • Remove a check-in: remove a mistaken entry.

9. Archiving and deleting sessions

  • Click … next to a session on the dashboard.
  • Archive hides it from Your sessions but keeps everything. Find it under Archived and use Unarchive to bring it back. Archiving a running session closes it without sending the report email.
  • Delete removes the session and all its check-ins permanently.

10. What students do

  1. Open the phone camera and point it at the QR code, then tap the link.
  2. The first time, sign in with Google{% if domains %} using their {{ domains }} account{% endif %}. The phone remembers it, so later check-ins take a single scan.
  3. A green tick with the time confirms the check-in. A confirmation email follows if you chose that.

11. Common problems

"QR code expired"
The student opened an old code, for example from a photo. Ask them to scan the screen again.
"Check-in is paused"
Check-in is paused. Click Resume.
"This check-in is closed"
Another check is now open, or the session is closed. Open the right check on the display, or add the student by hand.
"Not in an allowed email domain" / "Account not allowed"
The student signed in with a personal account. They should sign out and use{% if domains %} their {{ domains }} account{% else %} their institution account{% endif %}.
"Signing in took too long"
The scan is kept for 3 minutes while a student signs in. They should scan again.
A student has no phone
Add them by hand on the attendance list.

12. For administrators

Settings (top right) controls the institution name, logos, accent colour, default language, allowed student email domains, teachers (by domain or one by one), time zone, how often the QR code changes, and whether new sessions email students and teachers by default. Changes take effect immediately.
